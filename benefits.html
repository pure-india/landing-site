The Science

Backed by Research

We're not just repeating herbal folklore — here's some of the early clinical research behind spearmint tea and hormonal health.

Phytotherapy Research · 2007

Effect of spearmint teas on androgen levels in women with hirsutism

Akdoğan et al. gave 21 women with hirsutism (12 of them with PCOS) spearmint tea twice a day for 5 days. Free testosterone dropped significantly, while LH, FSH and estradiol went up.

Read the study on PubMed →

Phytotherapy Research · 2010 · Randomized Controlled Trial

Spearmint herbal tea has significant anti-androgen effects in PCOS

In a 30-day trial, Grant compared spearmint tea to a placebo in 42 women with PCOS. The spearmint group saw a significant drop in free and total testosterone, and reported feeling better about their hirsutism.

Read the study on PubMed →

These are small, early-stage clinical studies (21–42 participants) — not large-scale trials, and not a guarantee of results. They're shared for informational purposes only.
