Pure India began with a simple frustration: most "herbal" teas on the shelf were mostly dust, fillers and added flavouring. We wanted something closer to what grows in the ground — whole leaves, dried gently, with nothing else added.

Our first tea, Spearmint, is sourced from small farms across India and handled by people who've been drying herbs the same careful way for years. Every batch is small enough that we can vouch for what's inside the jar.

We're just getting started — one tea today, many more herbal blends on the way — but the promise stays the same: pure ingredients, honestly labelled, made in India.
