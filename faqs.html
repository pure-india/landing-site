Usage & Storage

Steep 1 teaspoon of leaves per cup in water just off the boil (95°C) for 4–5 minutes, then strain and enjoy.

Each 100g jar makes 120+ cups. Stored well, the tea stays fresh for up to 12 months after opening.

Keep the jar tightly sealed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
